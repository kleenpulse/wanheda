## Decisions made

- **The run uses tab-level offline, not OS-level.** OS-level offline cuts the Claude session. Tab offline blocks every request from the page and its workers.
- **Book follow uses the scroll view's rule.** The line goes under the top bar once it leaves the view, or once it comes within 2 lines of the foot bar.
- **V47's 60-character first unit stays (user's choice):** gap safety on a throttled tab matters more than about 0.6 s of first audio. A shorter first unit would need the real RTF remembered across plays and a lower budget floor everywhere. Not done.
- **One parsed document is kept in memory** (the one open in the reader). Its cost is the parsed sentences, about the same as `held` while speaking.

## Known problems and risks

- **Piper offline needs its worker loaded once while online, on this build.** There's no service worker, so a fresh deploy opened offline falls back to Web Speech. Phase 8 (desktop) or a service worker fixes it.
- **A press soon after Stop or a seek waits for the cancelled unit.** A cancel only drops a job that hasn't started, and the onnxruntime run can't be interrupted. Measured: 837–1315 ms extra at 1.5 s after a Stop. The worst case is one full unit (≤ 200 characters, about 3 s). Fix ideas: a second worker (twice the memory), smaller later units, or recycling the worker on cancel (model reload 2–3 s). Not done; it's outside V50's scenario.
- **A flaky test:** one failure in 8 full runs, never caught by name.
- **The pre-warm and the parse wait for `ensureText`.** On a first open of a huge book, that is longer than 45 s.
- **The idle parse is still a long task (87 ms)**, just no longer at the press. A worker parse would remove it, but the structured clone back costs about the same. Not done.
- **A 51–71 ms task at the press** (render of the reader, island and dock on play), before the first audio.
- **"End of chapter" on this book means a whole Saga** (the outline has one root, so chapters are its children, V49). It wasn't exercised.
- **The markdown book** has its own `bring` (text pages are cut to fit the stage). It wasn't affected by B22 and wasn't re-run.
- **Left in the MCP Chrome profile, origin :3000:** localStorage `wanheda:speech-log = "1"`, and the book's bytes plus text.json in OPFS (the library already listed it). The scratchpad holds a copy of the PDF.
